Stage 01 / 05Week 1–2

Site survey & soil test

Before anyone draws a line, we test the ground. Boreholes and a plate load test tell the structural engineer what the soil can carry, so the foundations are sized for your plot, not a rule of thumb.

3 boreholes · plate load test · report in 7 days

Stage 02 / 05Week 2–8

Design & approvals

Architectural and structural drawings are coordinated in-house, then submitted to LDA, DHA or your society. You sign a fixed-price contract against a full bill of quantities before we break ground.

Drawing set · BOQ · LDA / DHA submission · fixed price

Stage 03 / 05Month 2–7

Grey structure

Footings, RCC frame and slabs, one floor at a time. Every pour is cube-tested and every bar schedule is checked against the drawings before the concrete truck arrives.

C25 concrete · Grade 60 steel · 7 & 28-day cube tests

Stage 04 / 05Month 7–13

Finishing

Brickwork, plaster, glazing, flooring, electrics and plumbing. A single site manager runs every trade on one schedule, so no crew sits waiting on another.

One site manager · weekly walk-through with you

Stage 05 / 05Month 14

Handover

We walk the building with you, close every item on the snag list and hand over as-built drawings. After that we stay on call: the structure carries our ten-year warranty.

Snag list closed · as-built drawings · 10-year structural warranty

Sheet S-001Site standards

The spec sheet we build to.

Every RS site works to the same written standard. Ask any contractor for theirs before you sign, including us.

Concrete

C25 (3,600 psi) minimum for the frame, batched and slumped on site.

CheckCubes crushed at 7 & 28 days, every pour

Steel

Grade 60 deformed bars, mill-certified, stored off the ground.

CheckBar schedule signed off before each pour

Brickwork

First-class burnt clay bricks, 9 × 4.5 × 3 in, soaked before laying.

CheckRandom batch absorption test

Curing

Seven days minimum on every slab and column, ponded or wet-covered.

CheckLogged daily by the site engineer

Waterproofing

Roof and wet areas: two-coat system plus membrane, lapped up walls.

Check48-hour ponding test before tiling

Reporting

Photo log of the day's work sent to you every evening.

CheckWeekly site visit with your engineer
